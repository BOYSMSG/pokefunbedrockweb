⚠️ REFUND POLICY

All purchases are final and non-refundable.
By completing a purchase on the Pokedrock SMP Store, you agree to our store terms and refund policy.
Any attempt to perform an unauthorized chargeback or payment reversal may result in a permanent ban from Pokedrock SMP, our associated services, and/or our stores.
Payments are processed and secured through Tebex, a trusted platform for gaming communities and online transactions.

⏱️ PURCHASE DELIVERY

Most purchases are delivered automatically to your Minecraft Bedrock account.
Please allow up to 1–20 minutes for your purchase to appear in-game.
If you have not received your purchase after this period:

  • Make sure you entered the correct Minecraft Bedrock Gamertag.
  • Restart Minecraft or reconnect to the server.
  • Check that you are connected to Pokedrock SMP.

If the purchase is still missing, open a support ticket on our Discord.
Provide your Tebex purchase receipt/order information so our staff can investigate.
